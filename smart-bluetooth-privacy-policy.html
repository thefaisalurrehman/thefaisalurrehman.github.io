Smart Bluetooth

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

Smart Bluetooth ("the app"), developed by Faisal ur Rehman, helps you discover, pair, connect, and manage Bluetooth devices, play selected photos, videos, and audio, and share added media with a browser using Cast to Web. This policy explains how the current version handles information. No account is required. The app currently has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or push-messaging services.

1. Information Used by the App

The app processes the following information to provide its features:

  • Bluetooth device information: Device names, Bluetooth addresses, device types, pairing and connection status, and signal strength where available. These support scanning, device lists, connections, favorites, and the home-screen widget.
  • Device information: Your device name, Bluetooth state, phone or tablet type, battery level, Android SDK version, and Android ID are read to display the Bluetooth Info screen. The Android ID is displayed locally and is not sent to the developer.
  • Selected media: Photos, videos, and audio you explicitly add through Android's photo or document picker, along with file names, types, and sizes, are accessed for display, playback, and optional local sharing.
  • Preferences: Theme and language choices, onboarding status, preferred device category, and favorite Bluetooth device addresses are retained on your device.
  • Local network information: Connection status, VPN status, and your phone's network IP address are used to display the Cast to Web address and connection guidance. Tapping the copy control places that address on your clipboard.

We do not operate a server that receives this app data. The app does not send your Bluetooth scans, Android ID, or selected media to the developer, and we do not sell or rent this information. Local sharing and Android backup are explained below.

2. Bluetooth and Location

On Android 12 and later, Nearby devices permissions allow Bluetooth scanning and connection. Scan results are not used to infer your physical location. On Android 11 and earlier, Android requires location permission and enabled location services for Bluetooth discovery. The app uses this access for discovery and does not request GPS coordinates or track location history.

Pairing and connection operations use Android's Bluetooth services. Android manages paired devices separately from the app; removing an app favorite does not unpair the device.

3. Photos, Videos, and Audio

You select the files the app may access using Android's system pickers. The app does not request broad storage or gallery access. It saves references to selected files and retains read access so you can use them again. It does not import a separate copy of the original file into an app-managed media library.

Removing an item from the app removes its saved reference and releases its retained read permission; it does not delete the original file. If you choose a file from a cloud-backed document or photo provider, that provider may retrieve it over the internet under its own policy.

4. Cast to Web and Local Sharing

Opening Cast to Web starts a web server on your phone. While it is active, all photos, videos, and audio added to the app, including their file names, types, and sizes, are available to browsers that can reach the server's address. Other devices may view, play, or download these files.

This feature is intended for your Wi-Fi or hotspot network. It uses unencrypted HTTP and has no password or sign-in. Anyone who can reach the server may access the added media. Use a trusted private network, avoid exposing the address through router forwarding or other network routes, and add only files you intend to share.

The feature serves media directly from your phone; it does not upload a copy to a developer-operated cloud service. Sharing stays active while the Cast to Web screen remains in the navigation stack, including when you open media screens from it or switch apps. Leaving that screen with Back, or tapping Stop in its notification when available, stops the server. Stopping sharing cannot remove copies already saved by another device.

5. Permissions and Their Purposes

Permission or accessPurpose
BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECTDiscover nearby devices and manage pairing and connections on Android 12+.
BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_ADMINBluetooth discovery and management on Android 11 and earlier.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATIONDeclared only for Android 11 and earlier to support Bluetooth discovery; the runtime flow requests fine location.
INTERNETRun the local HTTP server for Cast to Web.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_NETWORK_STATESupport local network connectivity and Cast to Web. Network and Wi-Fi state access is used to determine connectivity and the address shown to you.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CONNECTED_DEVICESupport ongoing audio playback and the local sharing service while the app is in the background.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShow playback controls and Cast to Web status and stop controls on supported Android versions.
System photo and document pickersGrant access to the individual media items you select, without broad file access.

You can manage runtime permissions in Android Settings → Apps → Smart Bluetooth → Permissions, or use Manage permissions on the app's privacy screen. Denying or revoking access may prevent related features from working. Notification settings are managed separately; denying notifications does not itself stop local sharing.

6. Storage, Backup, and Deletion

Preferences and selected media references are stored in the app's private storage. The configured Android backup and device-transfer rules include app preferences, which can contain favorite Bluetooth addresses, onboarding status, theme, and preferred category. These preferences may be backed up or transferred by Android depending on your device and backup settings. Selected media references are excluded from these app backup rules.

You can remove favorites and selected media entries in the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes its local app data; original media files and Android-managed Bluetooth pairings remain. Android may restore backed-up preferences after reinstallation. Manage backups through your device's Android or backup-provider settings. We do not have access to those backups.

7. Third-Party Services and Contact

The current app does not integrate Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Firebase Cloud Messaging. If future versions introduce services that change data handling, this policy will be updated to describe them.

Opening Google Play, choosing a cloud-backed media provider, or contacting support involves those external services under their own policies. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your enquiry. Avoid sending private media or unnecessary device identifiers. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through support, contact us so we can address it and delete it where appropriate.

9. Security

App preferences and media references use Android's app storage protections. Cast to Web has the local-network access limitations described above. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

Changes will appear on this page with a revised Last Updated date. Review this page when updating the app to understand any changes to its data practices.
